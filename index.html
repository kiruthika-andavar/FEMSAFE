ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology with a human purpose.

FemSafe explores how wearable technology could make it easier to seek help in uncomfortable or potentially unsafe situations. The proposed glove combines discreet interaction with connected features in a wearable form.

The project focuses on accessibility, quick activation, and a user-centred design. The features shown here describe the concept and may require hardware, connectivity, testing, and a companion application to work in practice.

✦

Our mission

Explore practical, discreet, and technology-enabled ways to support personal safety and confidence.
